## 1. What it is

Lumière Coffee is a small, recently opened café in **Valle de Ángeles**, Francisco Morazán, Honduras. The business presents itself as an elegant, warm specialty-coffee and breakfast spot. Its own copy stresses detail, authenticity, faith, and “small moments that light up the soul” — a play on *lumière*, French for light.

Public names used:

| Form | Where it appears |
| --- | --- |
| Lumière Coffee | Instagram captions and bio |
| Lumiére Coffee | Instagram display name; TikTok display name |
| Café Lumiere | Valle de Ángeles tourism Facebook/TikTok promotions |

It is **not** the same business as:

- Restaurante Lumière / Lumiere Hangout in Tegucigalpa (founded 1998, Multiplaza / Colonia Palmira)
- Restaurante La Casona Del Valle (Barrio Arriba, near the police post)
- Other cafés named Lumière in Mexico, Dubai, Branson (Missouri), Malaysia, Spain, France, etc.

## 2. Location

The café sits at **Hotel Boutique La Casona**, which the hotel itself says is inside the **Portal del Valle** commercial plaza.

| Source | Location text |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram bio (@lumierecoffee_hn) | Portal del Valle |
| Instagram / TikTok, 15 Sep 2026 | “Estamos ubicados en Hotel Boutique La Casona” |
| @valledeangeles.honduras and Facebook *Valle de Ángeles* | “Una cuadra abajo del parque, Portal del Valle, esquina opuesta a Plaza Mineral” |
| Hotel Facebook (@lacasonahotelhnd) | “13 habitaciones de lujo ubicadas en la nueva plaza comercial Portal del Valle” |
| Hotel website and plaza website | Barrio El Centro, 2nda Calle, Valle de Ángeles, Francisco Morazán, Honduras |

Those statements are consistent: Portal del Valle is a colonial-style plaza one block from the town park; Plaza Mineral (home of Varietal Coffee Roasters) is across the way; La Casona is a 13-room boutique hotel in that plaza; Lumière is the café there.

Approximate plaza coordinates from WorldPlaces (not a café-specific pin): **14.16171, -87.03455**.

Booking.com lists Boutique Hotel La Casona at Calle Hierro Barro Verde, 11101 Valle de Ángeles, and advertises a **coffee house on site**. OpenStreetMap Nominatim had **no** place named Lumiere Coffee as of this research.
