- **Not on TripAdvisor, Yelp, Foursquare, or Waze.**
- **No Google Maps listing could be found.** Maps requires JavaScript so this is not conclusive, but no indexed Google Maps URL surfaced for the business either, which is unusual for a listed venue.
- **No reviews of any kind** were found on any platform.
- **No news coverage** in Honduran media.

For a café that opened in May 2026, this is consistent with a business that has not yet claimed a Google Business Profile.

## 8. Two claims that did not hold up

1. **"Galería Sixtina inaugurated Café Lumiere on 27 May 2026."** An AI search summary asserted this. It could not be reproduced in any primary source. What *is* independently supported: Galería Sixtina is the gallery of Honduran painter Julio Visquerra and is reported to sit in Portal del Valle, and the café is in Portal del Valle. A shared address does not establish that the gallery opened the café, so treat any gallery connection as unverified.

2. **"The café has closed."** A 2026-09-23 TikTok from @valledeangeles.honduras reading "Esperamos que pronto esté abierto de nuevo 🙌🏻" surfaced in a search for the café. Fetching that video's embedded page data shows **no mention of Lumière anywhere in it**, so it is about some other venue. There is no evidence the café has closed; its most recent confirmed activity is the 15 September 2026 promotion.

### Method notes

Two things are worth recording for anyone repeating this:

- Direct scraping of Bing, Mojeek, Brave, Startpage and SearXNG from this environment returned **decoy or bot-challenge content** — Bing served results about Yahoo Mail and Juan Pardo for café queries. Results from those engines were discarded. DuckDuckGo's HTML endpoint, reached through a different egress path, returned correct results and became the main index.
- Instagram and TikTok profile pages are login-walled or JavaScript-only, but TikTok's **oEmbed endpoint and the `__UNIVERSAL_DATA_FOR_REHYDRATION__` JSON blob** in profile/video HTML are open, and search-engine snippets preserve full Instagram captions with like and comment counts. That combination produced essentially all of the primary data here.
